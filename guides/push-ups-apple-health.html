Save a PushCheck workout to Apple Health

By PushCheck · Published November 7, 2026 · Updated November 7, 2026

Apple Health export is free and optional. You choose which saved timed workouts to export. It sends the workout's start time and duration; your push-up count stays in PushCheck.

First, save a timed workout

  1. Open the Pro tab. The basic workout recorder is free.
  2. Start a workout, complete your session, enter your completed reps and save it.
  3. Open Workout history, or Apple Health and then Saved workouts.

A group check-in is a different record. Logging a rep total on Today does not create an Apple Health workout by itself. Saved workouts are held on this iPhone for your PushCheck account.

Choose the workout to export

  1. Find the saved workout and tap Save to Apple Health.
  2. If asked, review the Health permission request and allow Workouts.
  3. Wait for the workout to show Exported to Apple Health.

You can also open Apple Health in PushCheck and choose Enable workout export before exporting. Nothing exports automatically.

Avoid duplicate workouts

If your Watch or another app already saved the same workout to Health, skip the PushCheck export. PushCheck marks its own successful exports to help you avoid repeating them, but it cannot decide whether another app recorded the same effort.

If export is off

In Apple's Health app, open your profile, then Apps, then PushCheck, and allow Workouts. Return to PushCheck and try exporting the saved workout again. Your workout remains in PushCheck even if you do not grant Health permission.

If the device reports that Health is unavailable, use a supported iPhone with Health available. If an export fails, check its status before retrying. For help, contact jon@buddysautomation.com with your app version and the message shown.

What stays separate

  • Group check-ins: the counts and dates on your shared board.
  • Timed workouts: sessions saved on this iPhone, including received Watch workouts.
  • Apple Health: workout timing you explicitly export.

Deleting a workout from PushCheck does not delete an exported Health workout. Manage that record in Apple Health. Pro workout reports and Watch tools are optional additions; Health export itself does not require Pro.

Is it your kind of app?

PushCheck is for logging push-ups and keeping up with friends. Free core features include manual check-ins, private groups, shared leaderboards and streaks.

Optional Pro is a one-time purchase for public groups and challenge seasons. There are no ads or recurring subscriptions. Check the app for your regional price.

You enter your own reps. PushCheck does not count them automatically with a camera or sensor. Explore the FAQ before deciding.
